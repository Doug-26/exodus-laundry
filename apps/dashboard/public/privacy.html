Exodus Laundry Services — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

This policy explains what information the Exodus Laundry app (customer and rider) and staff dashboard collect, how it is used, and your choices. Exodus Laundry Services operates in Naga City, Philippines.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and mobile number, used to identify your orders and contact you.
  • Order details: service type, weight, price, notes, and status history.
  • Delivery address: for delivery orders, the map location and address note you confirm.
  • Rider location (riders only): while a rider has an active delivery, the app shares the rider's device location in the background — even when the app is closed or not in use — so the assigned customer can see the rider's live position and ETA.
  • Push notification token: to notify you when your laundry is ready.

How we use it

  • To process, track, and deliver your laundry orders.
  • To show the customer a live map and estimated arrival during delivery.
  • To send you order status notifications.

Location sharing & retention

Rider background location is shared only during an active delivery and is visible only to the assigned customer and Exodus staff. It stops the moment the rider marks the delivery as delivered, and the live-location record is deleted at that point. We do not sell location data or use it for advertising.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (authentication, database, hosting, messaging) and Google Maps / Routes to store data and provide maps and directions. Their handling of data is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke location and notification permissions in your device settings; core ordering still works.
  • You may request access to or deletion of your account data by contacting us.
